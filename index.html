✍️ Guided Practice: Triangle ABC

Triangle ABC with ∠A = 90°, AC = 6, AB = 8, BC = 10. Reference Angle: B.

Practice 1: What is sin(B)?

sin(B) = Opposite / Hypotenuse = 6 / 10 = 3/5 = 0.6

Practice 2: What is cos(B)?

cos(B) = Adjacent / Hypotenuse = 8 / 10 = 4/5 = 0.8

Practice 3: What is tan(B)?

tan(B) = Opposite / Adjacent = 6 / 8 = 3/4 = 0.75

🧠 HOTS: Angle Perspective Swap

Question: What happens if Student A chooses angle at ground (θ) and Student B chooses angle at top of wall (α)?

Answer: Opposite & Adjacent SWAP Positions!

The Hypotenuse NEVER changes because it is always directly across from 90°. However, Opposite and Adjacent depend entirely on which acute angle you pick!
