Lesson 1 — Let's Review

Mean and Standard Deviation

Symbol Meaning Description
μ Mean Average or center of a distribution
σ Standard deviation Measures how spread out values are
X Raw score Actual score or value
Z Z-score Distance from the mean measured in standard deviations

Example

A test has a mean of 75 and a standard deviation of 5. The average score is 75. A score of 80 is 5 points above the mean, while 70 is 5 points below the mean.

Tagalog Note

Ang mean ang average o gitna ng scores. Ang standard deviation naman ang nagsasabi kung gaano kalayo o kalapit ang scores sa average. Kapag maliit ang standard deviation, magkakalapit ang scores.

Lesson 2 — Understanding the Z-Score

What is a Z-Score?

A Z-score tells how many standard deviations a particular value is away from the mean. It helps determine whether a value is above, below, or exactly at the mean.

+ Positive Z-Score

Value is above the mean (to the right of the center).

− Negative Z-Score

Value is below the mean (to the left of the center).

0 Z-Score of Zero

Value equals the mean (right at the center).

Tagalog Note

Ang positive at negative sign ay direction lang. Positive = lampas sa average; negative = mas mababa sa average. Hindi ibig sabihin na negative ang actual score.

Lesson 3 — Converting X into Z

From Raw Score (X) to Z-Score (Z)

Formula

z = (x − μ) ÷ σ
Step 1: Identify X, μ, and σ.
Step 2: Subtract the mean (μ) from the raw score (X).
Step 3: Divide the difference by the standard deviation (σ).
Step 4: Interpret the sign and size.
Worked Example — Above the Mean

Mean = 75, standard deviation = 5, raw score = 85.

Step 1: 85 − 75 = 10

Step 2: 10 ÷ 5 = 2

Answer: z = 2. The score is 2 standard deviations above the mean.

Worked Example — Below the Mean

Mean = 75, standard deviation = 5, raw score = 68.

z = (68 − 75) ÷ 5

z = −7 ÷ 5 = −1.4

Answer: z = −1.4, or 1.4 standard deviations below the mean.

Tagalog Note

Laging raw score minus mean! Kapag mas mababa ang score sa mean, negative ang subtraction. Normal lang iyon—huwag tanggalin ang negative sign.

Lesson 4 — Comparing Performances

Why Raw Scores Are Not Always Enough

Two students may take tests with different means and standard deviations. To compare their relative performance fairly, convert each score to a Z-score.

Worked Example: Maria vs. Jose

Maria

Score = 85, μ = 75, σ = 5

z = (85 − 75) ÷ 5 = 2

Jose

Score = 82, μ = 70, σ = 4

z = (82 − 70) ÷ 4 = 3

Conclusion: Jose performed better relative to his class because his Z-score (3) is higher than Maria's (2), even though Maria had a higher raw score!

Tagalog Note

Hindi raw score lang ang basehan. Isaalang-alang ang average at standard deviation ng grupo. Mas mataas na Z-score = mas mataas ang relative performance sa grupo.

Lesson 5 — Converting Z Back to X

From Z-Score (Z) Back to Original Value (X)

Formula

x = μ + zσ
Step 1: Multiply the Z-score by the standard deviation (z × σ).
Step 2: Add the result to the mean (μ).
Step 3: Write the answer in the original unit.
Worked Example — Positive Z-Score

Mean height = 160 cm, SD = 8 cm, z = 1.5.

Step 1: 1.5 × 8 = 12

Step 2: 160 + 12 = 172 cm

Answer: 172 cm.

Worked Example — Negative Z-Score

Mean weight = 55 kg, SD = 4 kg, z = −2.

Step 1: (−2) × 4 = −8

Step 2: 55 + (−8) = 47 kg

Answer: 47 kg.

Tagalog Note

Kapag negative ang Z-score, subtraction ang mangyayari dahil negative ang product. Pero ang actual value ay hindi kailangang negative.

Lesson 6 — Putting It Together

Which Formula Should You Use?

When Z is Requested

If X, mean (μ), and standard deviation (σ) are given and Z is requested:

z = (x − μ) ÷ σ
When X is Requested

If Z, mean (μ), and standard deviation (σ) are given and X is requested:

x = μ + zσ

Tagalog Note

Tingnan muna kung ano ang hinahanap. Kung raw score ang given at Z ang hinahanap, subtract then divide. Kung Z ang given at actual score ang hinahanap, multiply then add. Huwag pagpalitin ang formulas.
